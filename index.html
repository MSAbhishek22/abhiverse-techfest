THE ROAD

From first click
to final night

  1. January 2027

    Registrations open

    The gates swing wide. Pick your arena, name your team, and pin the dates to the wall — places in the flagship events are finite.

  2. February 2027

    Round 1 — online

    Problem statements drop at dawn. Builds, races and clashes run online, judged on working proof — never on promises.

  3. Late February 2027

    The shortlist

    The leaderboard settles. The finest teams from every arena receive the call — pack your bags, the finale is on home soil.

  4. March 2027

    The grand finale — India

    Two days on the ground: final builds, live races, the champions' podium, and a closing night nobody forgets in a hurry.
